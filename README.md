## Group 4 Project Idea

### Blockchain-Based Event Ticketing Platform

**Core Problem:** Event tickets are often duplicated, counterfeited, or resold at inflated prices. Organizers and buyers can struggle to verify whether a ticket is genuine and who currently owns it.

**Blockchain Technology integration:** The platform issues each ticket as a unique digital asset, creating a verifiable record of its issuance, ownership, and transfer. Organizers can set transfer and resale rules, such as limiting resale prices or collecting a fee(royaltee) on approved resales. Ticket checks can verify ownership at entry, while personal information and other sensitive data remain off-chain.

**Revenue Strategy:** Charge organizers a small fee per ticket sold, with optional subscription plans for features such as event analytics and ticket management. Collect a clearly disclosed service fee on approved resales, and optionally share resale royalties with organizers. Keep basic event creation accessible so fees scale with successful sales.
